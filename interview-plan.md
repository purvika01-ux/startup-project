# Interview plan: this week

Rules from earlier rounds:
- Introduce myself before asking for any numbers.
- Ask about a specific past event ("last time…"), not what people do in general.
- Don't name the problem before they do (e.g. don't say "dead stock" first).
- Write my own follow-ups based on what they just said.
- Always ask what they pay for today.

## 1. My father's shop (follow-up visit, round 3)

1. If you don't buy something and it becomes a hit, the shop next door sells it. When did that last happen, and what did you lose?
2. The register only shows the total. What would you want to know that it doesn't tell you?
3. What would have made your nephew's app work for you?
4. Your customers are college girls and housewives. Do they tell you what they want before you stock it? Tell me about the last time.
5. What do you pay for today to run the shop (apps, accounts help, the WhatsApp group, anything)? How much?
6. Can we count one shelf in the godown together, so we have a real number?

## 2. Shop owner #1 (referred by my father): _TBD name_

1. Tell me about your shop: what you sell and who buys.
2. On your last buying trip, how did you decide what to buy and how much?
3. What happened with that stock afterwards?
4. How do you find out an item has stopped selling?
5. What do you do with items that don't sell?
6. Have you tried anything to keep track of stock? What happened?
7. What do you pay for today to run the shop?

## 3. Shop owner #2 (referred by my father): _TBD name_

Same questions as shop owner #1. Compare the answers to see whether my father's problem is common.

## 4. Clinic: _TBD name_

1. Walk me through yesterday: how did patients get their turn?
2. What happens when the waiting room is full?
3. What do patients complain about most? When did that last happen?
4. Have you tried any token or appointment system? What happened?
5. Who handles the queue, and how much of their time does it take?
6. What do you pay for today for software or front desk help?

## 5. Cafe near GNDEC: _TBD name_

1. Walk me through your busiest hour yesterday: how did ordering and payment work?
2. When the queue gets long, what happens? Do people leave?
3. What do you use for billing today? What does it cost?
4. Have you looked at Petpooja or similar systems? What did you decide, and why?
5. If something breaks in your billing setup, who fixes it and how fast?
